3. Populate the Multi-Criteria Comparison Matrix across alternatives.
4. Pass the Uninformed Reader Test (understandable in 3 minutes) and guard against the 6 Anti-Patterns (God Decisions, Change Logs, Jargon Fortresses, Free Lunches, Strawmen, Implementation Manifestos).
